Everything you need, nothing you don't

One app on your PC, one on your phone. They find each other and just work.

File Sharing

Send files and folders between PC and phone at full Wi-Fi speed. No size limits, no compression, no waiting on uploads.

Clipboard Sync

Copy on your phone, paste on your PC — and the other way around. Text and links move instantly between devices.

Remote Mouse & Keyboard

Turn your phone into a wireless touchpad and keyboard. Control presentations or your media PC from the couch.

PC Power Control

Lock, sleep, restart or shut down your Windows PC straight from your phone. Never get up mid-movie again.

PIN Pairing + TLS

Devices pair with a one-time PIN and pin each other's TLS certificates. Every session is encrypted end to end on your LAN.

No Cloud, Ever

Traffic never leaves your Wi-Fi. No servers, no accounts, no sign-ups, no analytics phoning home. Your files stay yours.

Up and running in a minute

1

Install both apps

Grab the Windows installer and the Android APK from the release page. Install on the same Wi-Fi network.

2

Pair with a PIN

Open VrShare on both devices, pick your PC from the list, and confirm the one-time PIN. Certificates are pinned automatically.

3

Share away

Drop files, sync your clipboard, drive your mouse, or power off your PC — all from your phone, all on your LAN.

Built for people who own their data

VrShare is a free, open-source alternative to cloud-based sharing tools. It was built for one simple reason: moving a file between two devices sitting three metres apart shouldn't require uploading it to a server on another continent.

The Windows app is written in Rust (Tauri v2) and the Android app in Kotlin — small, fast, and native. The protocol is documented and open, so anyone can audit how it works.

Frequently asked questions

Is VrShare really free?

Yes. VrShare is free for non-commercial use under the PolyForm-Noncommercial-1.0.0 license. There are no premium tiers, no ads, and no feature gates.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. VrShare works entirely on your local Wi-Fi network. Your router doesn't even need internet access — just both devices on the same LAN.

Is my data private?

Your files never leave your network. Devices pair with a one-time PIN, pin each other's TLS certificates, and every transfer is encrypted. There are no accounts and no telemetry.

Which devices are supported?

Windows 10/11 (64-bit) and Android 8.0 or newer. Both devices must be on the same Wi-Fi network to discover each other.

Where do I report a bug?

Open an issue on the GitHub repository or email TurabDev@proton.me.
